# Duer Product Showcase

A looping, animated preview of **Duer** — AI-Powered Operations Management Platform.

![Duer Demo](duer-demo.gif)

Duer was built to solve a common operational challenge: keeping track of recurring responsibilities, critical deadlines, vendor renewals, operational spending, and system health across multiple tools. The platform centralizes operational workflows into a single intelligent workspace, enabling users to manage due items, monitor business-critical activities, visualize operational health, and leverage AI for faster decision-making.

## What it does

* Centralized dashboard for operational tasks and due items
* Intelligent tracking of overdue, upcoming, and recurring responsibilities
* Calendar and timeline views for planning and operational visibility
* Operational spend and budget monitoring
* License, subscription, and vendor renewal management
* System health monitoring with risk awareness
* AI-powered natural language assistant for operational queries and insights
* Analytics and reporting for productivity and operational performance

## Built with

* Supabase (Authentication, Database & Realtime)
* PostgreSQL
* JavaScript
* AI-powered workflows
* Operational analytics and reporting
* Responsive, mobile-friendly interface
